## Proof caveats
- Old Mutual and SanlamAllianz prove insurer embedding in Kenya. They do not prove results for banks.
- No Kenyan case study with metrics exists. Do not imply results beyond launch and offering.
- Never state a response time as a promise. Always "stated average, subject to responder availability".

## Banned

### Banned angles
- Any guarantee of response time.
- The 20 to 60 minute public delay comparison (unverified, dropped from C9).
- Price, discounts or "free".
- Claims about 922 integration or AURA's stance on 922 (Not found).
- Attacking Rescue.co or its ambulance numbers.
- Diagnosing the prospect's team as failing (iron law 14).

### Banned words (company-specific)
- "guaranteed", "guarantee" (also globally banned).
- Naming any customer not in the table above.

### Never in copy
- Contract values, churn, pipeline or reply-rate data, the internal strategy files.
