# Messaging

Sources: context/docs/research and context/icp/personas.md. Check each figure against its source before use.

## Core message
Give your customers real-world help in an emergency, under your own brand, through one integration. (company.md, voice.md)

## Pillars

**1. One platform, three kinds of help.** Security, medical and roadside response together (company.md).
Proof: People Protect, Property Protect, Global SOS (company.md); Rescue.co sells roadside and air as add-ons (competitors.md).

**2. Your brand, our network.** White-label app, API and panic buttons; users see your brand (company.md).
Proof: ICEA Lion users see ICEA Lion (company.md); OM Rescue Plus and SanlamAllianz Emergency Assist (customers.md).

**3. Vetted responders, visible incidents.** Licence and credential checks, training, response time monitoring (voice.md); live GPS and a customer platform (company.md).
Proof: PSRA accreditation required for Kenyan security responders (company.md); Glovo: "full transparency to emergency response for the first time" (customers.md); more than 2,000 vetted responders in Kenya (company.md).

**4. Proven with brands you know.** Old Mutual, SanlamAllianz, Uber, Glovo, Greenwheels (customers.md); Tracker: "relatively easy to integrate" (customers.md); 10 minute average response, AURA's figure (company.md).

## Angle by persona
- **Champion (operations owner):** see and handle every incident without building a network or phone-chasing responders. Lead with Glovo incident tracking, easy integration and the audit trail (customers.md, personas.md).
- **Economic buyer (executive sponsor):** add a branded safety service customers notice. Lead with Old Mutual extending OM Rescue Plus, SanlamAllianz and the per-user model (customers.md, company.md). Do not quote a price.
- **Influencer (security and compliance lead):** vetted, licensed responders and a documented API while PSRA rules tighten (company.md, market.md, signals.md).

## Words to use
From AURA's copy (voice.md): responder, response partner, network, platform, alert, panic button, control room, integration, white-label, nationwide; vetted, trusted, verified, real-time, on-demand, rapid; embed, integrate, connect, dispatch, protect. Add concrete Kenyan scenes such as riders, motorists and Nairobi roads (voice.md, INFERRED).

## Words and claims to avoid
- Response time promises or guarantees (company.md, voice.md).
- Lives saved, safe places, alerts or funding dates until conflicts are resolved (company.md).
- Any price or "free" (company.md: no price list).
- Claims about 922 integration or AURA's stance (competitors.md, market.md: Not found).
- Calling any Kenyan customer a case study (customers.md: none exists).
- Vague abstractions such as "protection" without a scene (voice.md, INFERRED).
- Em dashes.
