# AURA: company research

Raw research, not conclusions. Every fact has a source URL and the date checked (2026-11-13). Anything inferred is marked INFERRED. Missing items say "Not found". Quotes are short exact fragments; the linked page has the full wording.

Scope note: AURA here means the emergency response platform at aurasos.com (formerly aura.services). Search results also surface an unrelated "AURA" holding company (leadiq.com listing). Ignore it.

## What they sell

- AURA describes itself as a "B2B emergency response platform" that helps businesses embed real-world response into their products and services, connecting end users to security and medical responders. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke/about | 2026-11-13]
- Business model is embedded response, not direct-to-consumer: the About page tells individuals to find an AURA-powered partner through the Partner Hub. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke/about | 2026-11-13]
- Press describes it as B2B2C: a per-user, per-month subscription fee, sold through resellers and distributors such as insurers, security companies, monitoring centres and app providers, via white-labelled apps and panic buttons. [src: https://techcabal.com/2025/05/16/south-africa-founded-aura-raises-14-6-million/ | 2026-11-13]
- AURA Kenya's head of operations also called it a B2B2C platform and said it works through businesses to push the net cost per user as low as possible. [src: https://standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/tech-innovation/article/2001542043/how-ambulance-shortage-deepens-inequality-in-care-in-slums | 2026-11-13] (article date not shown on page)
- End users of partner products interact under the partner's brand, not AURA's (example given: ICEA Lion Group). [src: same Standard article | 2026-11-13]

## Products and services (from the Kenya homepage navigation and sections)

- People protect: on-demand personal safety and emergency response, with three response types: security response, medical response, roadside assistance. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- Property protect: verified alarm response for properties, including "security protect" for rapid alarm response by private security. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- Global SOS: 24/7 worldwide emergency response, pitched as multilingual incident management plus local response across markets. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- Scheduled services: planned security services and scheduled support (link points to the South Africa site). [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- Technology layer, listed on the homepage: custom API integration, white-label mobile app, customer platform (manage users or sites, monitor alerts, insights), dispatching (licensed responders, live GPS, secure messaging) and scheduling (booking engine for guard response). [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- Response partner technology: operational tools for response companies. Enablement: customer onboarding and responder onboarding. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- Developer docs describe the "Aura Panic API": onboard customers, manage subscriptions, configure fixed locations, create and update callouts, receive webhooks, connect selected device or gateway flows. Production access needs AURA sign-off after staging tests. [src: https://docs.aurasos.com/overview/introduction | 2026-11-13]
- Three integration paths in the docs: People Protect, Property Protect, White-label App. [src: https://docs.aurasos.com/overview/introduction | 2026-11-13]
- Dispatch flow as AURA shows it: alert reaches the 24/7 control room, responder matched by location, nearest partner dispatched, responder arrives and reports back. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- Navigation upgrade built with Google Maps Platform, promoted in a site-wide banner; AURA says it rebuilt its responder app in H1 2026 and integrated Google Maps navigation. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke ; https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/tech-innovation/article/2001558173/tech-driven-emergency-response-gains-ground-as-platform-targets-gaps-in-public-system | 2026-11-13]
- AURA's terms state response availability depends on responders being present in an area and that response times cannot be guaranteed. [src: same Standard article, reporting the terms | 2026-11-13] (terms page itself not read)
- Industry verticals listed on the site: facility management, property management, construction and property, alarm monitoring centres, telematics, security and guarding, consumer technology apps, handheld devices, employee wellness, medical insurer, ATM protection, financial services, lone worker. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]

## Pricing model

- Per-user, per-month subscription fee paid through partners (B2B2C). [src: https://techcabal.com/2025/05/16/south-africa-founded-aura-raises-14-6-million/ | 2026-11-13]
- Partners can offer the service to staff or customers as a free or paid value-added service. [src: https://aura.services/ke/?p=34 | 2026-11-13] (older domain, undated)
- The geo-banner on aurasos.com says regional pricing sits on the local site. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
- 2022 consumer-facing copy said the monthly subscription fee fully covers a private ambulance and transport to the nearest hospital. [src: https://techweez.com/2022/11/11/aura-affordable-emergency-response-services-kenya/ | 2026-11-13]
- Published price list, per-user fee in KES or USD, API usage fees, onboarding fees: Not found.

## Markets and footprint

- Operates in South Africa, Kenya, the UK and the US. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke/about | 2026-11-13]
- Timeline on the About page: founded 2017; Series A 2020; Kenya and UK launched 2021; Series B 2024; US and Texas launched 2025. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke/about | 2026-11-13] (see Conflicts: dates differ from press)
- Kenya: Nairobi office. AURA says Kenya network "expanding safety innovation in East Africa". [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke/about | 2026-11-13]
- Kenya network size: "more than 2,000 vetted responders" (Star, 16 June 2026). An older AURA Kenya page says 2,500 or more. [src: https://www.the-star.co.ke/sasa/technology/2026-06-16-glovo-riders-to-benefit-from-real-time-emergency-response-system ; https://aura.services/ke/?p=34 | 2026-11-13]
- Kenya coverage: dozens of private response companies in more than 40 cities and towns. [src: https://newstrends.co.ke/kenyan-innovation-bringing-faster-emergency-response-to-millions/?amp=1 | 2026-11-13]
- Kenya coverage with full device technology on response vehicles: about 70 percent of major urban areas, per AURA's Kenya head of operations. [src: https://standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/tech-innovation/article/2001542043/how-ambulance-shortage-deepens-inequality-in-care-in-slums | 2026-11-13] (article date not shown)
- Kenya launch coverage named Nairobi and Mombasa, partnering with KK Security (GardaWorld), led by general manager Hilary Itela. [src: https://disrupt-africa.com/2021/03/17/sa-cloud-based-security-startup-aura-launches-in-kenya/ ; https://securitysa.com/12996r | 2026-11-13]
- Kenya response partner requirements: PSRA accreditation, valid security service provider licence, public liability insurance (security); six requirements for medical. [src: https://aura.services/ke/?p=26 | 2026-11-13]
- Kenya medical responders must be verified through bodies such as the Nursing Council of Kenya or the Kenya Council of Emergency Medical Technicians. [src: https://www.aura.services/ke/en/medical-protection | 2026-11-13]
- Upcoming events on the Kenya homepage are all in the United States (Oct to Nov 2026). No Kenya event listed. [src: https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
