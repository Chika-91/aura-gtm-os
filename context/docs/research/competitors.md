# AURA: competitor research

Raw research, not conclusions. Every fact has a source URL and the date checked (2026-11-13). Anything inferred is marked INFERRED. Missing items say "Not found". Quotes are short exact fragments; the linked page has the full wording.

Scope: Kenya first, because the target page is aurasos.com/en-ke. Global players are listed separately as adjacent. AURA does not publish a competitor list: Not found.

## Direct competitor in Kenya: Rescue.co (Flare)

- What it is: a Kenya-based emergency dispatch platform, founded by Caitlin Dolkart, run on its own "Flare" technology. [src: https://test.capitalfm.africa/rescue-co-cuts-emergency-response-time-to-as-little-as-two-minutes/ | 2026-11-13] (founding year given as 2016 here; a peer-reviewed case study says launched 2017)
- Scale claims: more than 800 ambulances, plus security teams and roadside providers; response times of 2 to 15 minutes, down from an average 163 minutes. [src: same Capital FM page | 2026-11-13]
- Independent case study (PMC): onboarded more than 800 public and private ambulance providers and coordinated more than 47,000 emergency responses across all 47 counties by 2025; average response 13 minutes urban, under 30 rural. [src: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12418930 | 2026-11-13] (authorship and any Rescue.co involvement not checked)
- Positioning: its launch announcement describes Rescue.co as East Africa's leading emergency response network (paraphrased; exact wording in the quote section below). [src: https://newstrends.co.ke/?p=144785 | 2026-11-13]
- Roadside product: "Roadside by rescue.co" at KES 8,888 including VAT per vehicle per year, aimed at drivers, fleets, insurers and businesses, built on Flare, with more than 200 roadside specialists. [src: https://newstrends.co.ke/?p=144785 ; https://tech-ish.com/2025/05/21/rescue-co-roadside-insurance-kenya/ | 2026-11-13] (launch reported May 2025)
- Roadside partners named: Glovo, Bolt, ICEA Lion, JW Seagon, plus other corporates, fleets and micro-insurers. [src: https://newstrends.co.ke/?p=144785 | 2026-11-13]
- OVERLAP with AURA: Glovo and ICEA Lion appear in both AURA's and Rescue.co's partner lists. Whether the products are used together or side by side: Not found.
- Consumer pricing (older, undated): subscription from about $32 per year for an individual and about $103 for a family of four. [src: https://www.howwemadeitinafrica.com/building-kenyas-uber-for-ambulances/151182/ | 2026-11-13]
- Integrations: linked to Bolt so responders can pinpoint a caller's location, per Capital FM. [src: https://test.capitalfm.africa/rescue-co-cuts-emergency-response-time-to-as-little-as-two-minutes/ | 2026-11-13]
- Insurer pilot: Flare worked with Jubilee Health Insurance. [src: https://www.techemerge.org/pilots/flare-jubilee-health-insurance | 2026-11-13]
- Hiring (signal of ops investment): Rescue.co lists Nairobi dispatch roles on Greenhouse, for example Emergency Dispatch Shift Lead. [src: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/rescue/jobs/4070722009 | 2026-11-13] (posting date not shown)
- Business model difference, INFERRED: Rescue.co sells membership plans and its own branded service (annual cover, short-term evacuation) as well as platform access, while AURA says it sells through partners under their brand. Verify against both sites.

## Government: SHA 922 Lifeline and National Ambulance Dispatch Centre

- Launched by President Ruto on 5 August 2026 as Kenya's single national emergency medical number, coordinating ambulances in all 47 counties from the Social Health Authority headquarters in Nairobi. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/president-ruto-launches-922-lifeline-to-cut-emergency-response-times-nationwide ; https://gaa.go.ke/node/4419 | 2026-11-13]
- Calls are free from any mobile network, even without airtime. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/ruto-says-922-emergency-calls-will-be-free-across-all-mobile-networks | 2026-11-13]
- Ambulance evacuation and the first 24 hours of emergency treatment are paid from the Emergency, Chronic and Critical Illness Fund, whether or not the patient is registered with SHA. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/ruto-unveils-free-emergency-care-plan-under-new-922-lifeline | 2026-11-13]
- SHA has contracted 1,243 ambulance and emergency service providers; more than 200 KMPDC-accredited ambulances were integrated at launch. [src: https://thekenyatimes.com/health/emergency-medical-calls/ | 2026-11-13]
- Partners in building it: SHA, Kenya Red Cross, ambulance providers and Safaricom. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/kenya-set-to-launch-national-922-ambulance-command-centre-to-speed-up-emergency-response | 2026-11-13]
- Scope: medical evacuation only at launch; security and roadside are not part of 922. The Standard says the initial rollout moves patients from the scene to the nearest emergency facility. [src: https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001552876/struggle-for-ambulances-leaves-lives-at-risk-as-kenya-unveils-emergency-response-plan | 2026-11-13]

## Adjacent global players (no Kenya presence found)

- RapidSOS (US): connects devices and apps to 911 and "trained Safety Agents" via APIs and SDKs; says human verification filters out 90%+ of non-critical alerts. Target buyers: wearables, smart home, connected health. [src: https://rapidsos.com/ ; https://rapidsos.com/enterprise/home-and-personal-safety/ | 2026-11-13]
- Noonlight (US): "a suite of emergency response APIs" for home, health and lifestyle products; says agents stop more than 99% of false alarms before contacting dispatch. [src: https://get.noonlight.com | 2026-11-13]
- Both route to public 911 centres, which differs from AURA's model of dispatching private security, medical and roadside responders. Relevant mainly for AURA's US push. [src: sources above; AURA model from https://www.aurasos.com/en-ke | 2026-11-13]
