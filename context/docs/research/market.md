# AURA: market and regulation research (Kenya focus)

Raw research, not conclusions. Every fact has a source URL and the date checked (2026-11-13). Anything inferred is marked INFERRED. Missing items say "Not found". Quotes are short exact fragments.

Caution on dates: several Kenyan news sites print the day's date in the page header, not the article date. Dates below come from the article text or URL where possible; otherwise the entry says "date not shown".

## 1. National emergency medical system (biggest recent change)

- 5 Aug 2026: President Ruto launched the SHA 922 Lifeline and the National Ambulance Dispatch Centre, one national emergency medical number covering all 47 counties, based at Social Health Authority headquarters in Nairobi. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/president-ruto-launches-922-lifeline-to-cut-emergency-response-times-nationwide ; https://allafrica.com/stories/202608060070.html | 2026-11-13]
- The government frames it as part of the Taifa Care and Universal Health Coverage programme. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/kenya-set-to-launch-national-922-ambulance-command-centre-to-speed-up-emergency-response | 2026-11-13]
- Funding: ambulance evacuation and the first 24 hours of emergency treatment are paid from the Emergency, Chronic and Critical Illness Fund at approved tariffs, regardless of SHA registration. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/ruto-unveils-free-emergency-care-plan-under-new-922-lifeline | 2026-11-13]
- Calls to 922 are free from any mobile network, even with no airtime. [src: https://www.dawan.africa/news/ruto-says-922-emergency-calls-will-be-free-across-all-mobile-networks | 2026-11-13]
- SHA says it has contracted 1,243 ambulance and emergency service providers; more than 200 KMPDC-accredited ambulances were integrated at launch; a real-time incident system links to the National Health Information Exchange. [src: https://thekenyatimes.com/health/emergency-medical-calls/ | 2026-11-13]
- Timeline slippage: in Oct 2025 the Health CS said the national ambulance response system would go live on 1 December 2025. It launched 5 Aug 2026. [src: https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-10-29-duale-national-ambulance-response-system-to-go-live-on-december-1 ; https://www.dawan.africa/news/president-ruto-launches-922-lifeline-to-cut-emergency-response-times-nationwide | 2026-11-13] (INFERRED: the launch slipped about eight months)
- 14 July 2026: SHA called ambulance owners and operators to training for the Ambulance Evacuation Programme. [src: https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/125226-sha-begins-preparations-rollout-ambulance-evacuation-programme | 2026-11-13]
- Deadline: KMPDC extended free ambulance registration and licensing to 30 October 2026 (already past on 2026-11-13). Any further extension: Not found. [src: https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127143-kmpdc-extends-free-ambulance-registration-deadline-october-30 | 2026-11-13]
- Before the launch, reporting described no coordinated national ambulance dispatch, outdated county communication methods and no real-time tracking in many counties. [src: https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/125226-sha-begins-preparations-rollout-ambulance-evacuation-programme | 2026-11-13]
- SHA scope at launch is medical evacuation from the scene to the nearest emergency facility; county ambulances stay the backbone, with SHA doing coordination and dispatch technology. [src: https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001552876/struggle-for-ambulances-leaves-lives-at-risk-as-kenya-unveils-emergency-response-plan | 2026-11-13]
- What this means for AURA: INFERRED, not sourced. It could reduce the "no one to call" gap AURA's medical pitch relies on, and it could create demand for the security and roadside layers that 922 does not cover. Neither effect is evidenced yet. AURA's own response: Not found.

## 2. The gap AURA's pitch addresses (before 922)

- Ambulance density: about one ambulance per 100,000 people on average, about one per 250,000 in some Nairobi slums, against a WHO recommendation of one per 50,000. [src: https://standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/tech-innovation/article/2001542043/how-ambulance-shortage-deepens-inequality-in-care-in-slums | 2026-11-13] (date not shown)
- Medical response times often run 20 to 60 minutes against a 15 to 20 minute national policy standard. [src: same Standard article | 2026-11-13]
- Rescue.co's case study puts the average ambulance response in Kenya at 162 minutes in 2017. [src: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12418930 | 2026-11-13]
- A family spent Sh6,000 on a private ambulance after the hospital had none available (anecdote). [src: https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001552876/struggle-for-ambulances-leaves-lives-at-risk-as-kenya-unveils-emergency-response-plan | 2026-11-13]
- AURA's own framing of the Kenya market: strong but fragmented private security and emergency medical industry. [src: https://africa.com/headlines/south-african-business-aura-expands-into-east-africa-with-new-kenyan-office | 2026-11-13] (2021)

## 3. Private security regulation (affects AURA's security responder supply and buyers' vendors)

- Private Security Regulation Act No. 13 of 2016 created the Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA), which licenses firms and guards, sets training and vets officers. [src: https://www.the-star.co.ke/counties/north-eastern/2025-11-23-new-regulations-to-streamline-operations-in-private-security-sector-official | 2026-11-13]
- 28 Mar 2026: PSRA shut 27 unlicensed security firms in a nationwide crackdown and asked Parliament to fast-track operational regulations. [src: https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-03-28-regulator-cracks-down-on-illegal-security-firms | 2026-11-13]
- 25 Aug 2026: PSRA published proposed regulations for public participation covering licensing, security equipment and a fund; one proposal requires guards to complete accredited training and assessment every year. [src: https://capitalfm.africa/kenya-moves-to-tighten-oversight-of-private-security-industry/ ; https://thekenyatimes.com/law-order/kenya-proposes-tougher-rules-for-private-security-guards/ | 2026-11-13] (comment deadline and adoption date: Not found)
- 6 Aug 2026: President Ruto directed PSRA, the Interior and Labour ministries to enforce minimum wage rules for private security; Citizen Digital reports a 15 percent wage rise and dropping the "watchman" label. [src: https://standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/national/article/2001554707/ruto-orders-security-firms-to-pay-minimum-wage-warns-of-crackdown ; https://citizen.digital/article/ruto-scraps-watchman-label-raises-pso-wages-15-percent-n387833 | 2026-11-13]
- Mid-Sept 2026: a PSRA CEO announced a nationwide crackdown on unqualified guards and unregistered companies. [src: https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/psra-begins-nationwide-crackdown-on-unregistered-security-companies/ | 2026-11-13] (PSRA leadership appears to have changed during 2026; another report calls the same CEO "former". Not verified.)
- Oct 2026: PSRA warned Kenya Railways that a security tender omitted mandatory compliance certifications; PSRA says the Guard Force Number is the sole proof of a guard's registration. [src: https://thekenyatimes.com/national/govt-warns-kenya-railways-on-security-tender/ | 2026-11-13] (page header shows 15 Oct 2026)
- Proposed amendment to allow guards to carry firearms in six high-risk situations (Bill 2024). Status: Not found. [src: eastleighvoice.co.ke article 50072, "PSRA proposes amendment of law to arm security guards in high-risk situations" (URL contains spaces; search the title) | 2026-11-13]
- Crackdowns are described as driving a shift to tech-led security. [src: https://standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/national/article/2001545527/crackdown-on-illegal-guards-drives-shift-to-tech-led-security | 2026-11-13] (16 Apr 2026)
- AURA requires its Kenyan security responders to hold PSRA accreditation, a security service provider licence and public liability cover. [src: https://aura.services/ke/?p=26 | 2026-11-13]
- INFERRED: tighter licensing and wage enforcement raise compliance and cost pressure on small security firms, which could push them toward platforms with vetting and dispatch, or shrink AURA's supply of compliant responders. No source states either effect.
