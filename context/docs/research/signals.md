# AURA: signals research

Raw research, not conclusions. This file lists public evidence that could show a company needs AURA now. "Observed" means a source states it. "INFERRED" means I connected it to AURA myself and no source makes that link. Every fact has a source URL and the date checked (2026-11-13). Missing items say "Not found".

Buyer types in scope, from AURA's own customer list: insurers, ride-hailing and delivery platforms, mobility and fleet operators, telematics providers, monitoring centres and security companies, employee-wellness and lone-worker programmes.
