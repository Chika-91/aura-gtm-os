## Where AURA wins
- **Breadth:** security, medical and roadside on one platform and API (company.md); Rescue.co sells roadside and air as add-ons (competitors.md).
- **Security response:** 922 covers medical evacuation only (competitors.md); AURA lists PSRA-accredited security responders (company.md).
- **Partner brand:** end users see the partner, for example ICEA Lion (company.md). Rescue.co also sells its own branded plans (competitors.md, INFERRED).
- **Regulated supply:** licence and credential vetting (company.md, voice.md) while PSRA enforcement tightens (market.md).
- **Kenyan references:** Old Mutual, SanlamAllianz, Glovo, Uber (customers.md).

## Where AURA loses
- **Medical scale and speed claims:** Rescue.co claims 2 to 15 minute responses and 800+ ambulances (competitors.md). AURA's ambulance count is Not found; its terms do not guarantee times (company.md).
- **Government backing:** 922 is free to the caller and the first 24 hours of care is state-funded (competitors.md, market.md).
- **Local proof:** no Kenyan case study; the Kenya page carries South Africa copy; events are all US (customers.md, signals.md, company.md).
- **Price transparency:** AURA has no published price. Rescue.co lists roadside at KES 8,888 per vehicle per year (competitors.md, company.md).

## How to respond
- **Rescue.co:** where an account uses it, pitch one platform with armed security, medical and roadside under one API, one SLA and one incident audit trail (competitors.md, INFERRED). Do not attack its ambulance numbers.
- **922:** position as the layer it does not cover: security and roadside (signals.md). AURA's own stance is Not found; confirm before writing copy.
- **Build in house:** point to the live white-label app, API and responder network (company.md).
- **Response time objection:** quote AURA's stated average with source; never guarantee (company.md, voice.md).
- **Shared accounts (Glovo, ICEA Lion):** ask how the services split today. Whether they run side by side is Not found (competitors.md).
