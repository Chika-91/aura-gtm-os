# Value proposition by persona

Personas come from context/icp/personas.md. Proofs come from context/docs/research (company.md, customers.md, market.md, signals.md, voice.md, competitors.md). INFERRED means no source states the link. Not found means research has no evidence. Research has no buyer interviews, so pains are mostly paraphrases, not buyer quotes.

## 1. Champion: operations owner
Titles seen: Head of Operations (Greenwheels), Operations Manager (Glovo Kenya), COO (Tracker). Added, INFERRED: Head of Product, Claims, Customer Care, VAS or Bancassurance, and divisional insurance heads (personas.md).

- **Job to be done (INFERRED):** protect riders, drivers or policyholders on the road and in emergencies without building a response network, and see every incident through to the end.
- **Pain:**
  - Integration effort. Tracker COO: "relatively easy to integrate" (customers.md).
  - No end-to-end view of an incident. AURA's Kenya lead, not the buyer: "full transparency to emergency response for the first time" (customers.md).
  - Riders or drivers exposed to road risk (Glovo Operations Manager, paraphrased, customers.md).
  - Customers wait when ambulances are scarce: about 1 per 100,000 people against a WHO 1 per 50,000 (market.md). Typical medical response 20 to 60 minutes against a 15 to 20 minute policy standard (market.md).
  - Care teams phone-chase fragmented responders; claims lack scene audit trails (personas.md, INFERRED).
- **Gain:** security, medical and roadside response with incident tracking visible to the ops team (customers.md). Greenwheels credits AURA with better operational efficiency and customer experience (customers.md, paraphrased). Portal with full audit trail and customised reporting at OUTsurance (customers.md, paraphrased).
- **Proof:** Glovo Kenya announced 16 June 2026. Uber in-app emergency button. Greenwheels testimonial. AURA Kenya states 10 to 15 minutes for ambulances and about 10 minutes for security in Nairobi (company.md, AURA-stated, not independently verified).

## 2. Economic buyer: executive sponsor
Titles seen: Managing Director General Insurance (Old Mutual); CEO (SanlamAllianz General Insurance Kenya, OUTsurance, Samsung Mobile SA). Added, INFERRED: CFO, CHRO (personas.md).

- **Job to be done (INFERRED):** add a branded value-added emergency service that improves customer safety and well-being at a cost per user the business can carry.
- **Pain:**
  - Improving customer safety and well-being through technology. Old Mutual MD: "leveraging technology in enhancing the safety and well-being of our customers" (customers.md).
  - Cost per user across a large base. AURA's Kenya lead says working through businesses pushes net cost per user down (company.md).
  - No public price, so unit economics cannot be modelled yet (company.md).
  - Build-it-yourself burden (personas.md, INFERRED).
- **Gain:** emergency response embedded in policies or products under the partner's brand; end users see the partner, not AURA (company.md). Free or paid value-added service both supported (company.md). Old Mutual planned to extend beyond motor private (customers.md).
- **Proof:** OM Rescue Plus launched 29 Jan 2025 and its page now lists motor, medical and SME health. SanlamAllianz Emergency Assist across motor, home, WIBA, SME and personal accident. ICEA Lion named as client (customers.md, signals.md). Tracker: revenue-driving value-added service, about 3x return, South Africa, basis not published (customers.md).

## 3. Influencer: security and compliance lead
Titles seen: Senior Manager, Security Operations (IHS). Added, INFERRED: Head of Innovation, Legal, CTO, Chief Risk Officer (personas.md).

- **Job to be done (INFERRED):** confirm the vendor's responders are vetted and compliant, and that the integration and liability terms are safe to sign.
- **Pain:**
  - Personnel who are well trained, disciplined and responsive (IHS, paraphrased, customers.md).
  - Vendor licensing exposure: PSRA shut 27 unlicensed firms on 28 Mar 2026; draft regulations 25 Aug 2026 (market.md). PSRA warned Kenya Railways over a tender lacking compliance certificates, Oct 2026 (signals.md).
  - Uncertain responder availability by area; times not guaranteed (company.md).
- **Gain:** responders vetted for licensing and credentials, trained and monitored on response times (voice.md). Kenyan security responders must hold PSRA accreditation, a security licence and public liability cover; medical responders are verified through bodies such as the Nursing Council of Kenya (company.md). Documented Panic API with staging then AURA sign-off (company.md).
- **Proof:** IHS testimonial; Tracker "relatively easy to integrate"; more than 2,000 vetted responders in Kenya (company.md, older page says 2,500+).
