## Positioning statement

For Kenyan insurers, mobility platforms and other businesses that want to offer emergency help to their own customers, AURA is the B2B emergency response platform that embeds security, medical and roadside response under the partner's brand through one integration. Unlike a single-service provider or a network built in house, AURA offers vetted responders, live dispatch tracking and an incident audit trail on one platform, and it already powers products at Old Mutual, SanlamAllianz, Glovo and Uber in Kenya (customers.md).
