# Brand

Drawn only from context/docs/research/voice.md. Research found no brand guide, tone document, boilerplate, logo or colour specs, or banned-phrases list. This is AURA's observed voice, not an approved guide. Tone items are INFERRED in voice.md.

## Tone of voice
- Confident, benefit-first, mission-driven (voice.md, INFERRED).
- Short sentences, plain benefit headings, abstract nouns such as "protection" and "response" (voice.md, INFERRED).
- Values use superhero and warrior imagery, short imperatives and "we are" statements (voice.md).
- Kenyan press voice is more concrete: riders, motorists, Nairobi roads, ambulance times. Prefer it for Kenya copy (voice.md, INFERRED).
- Our rule: no em dashes, though AURA's own site uses them (voice.md).

## Key phrases from AURA's own copy
- "B2B emergency response platform" (About)
- "On-demand security response, powered by smart technology" (Kenya homepage H1)
- "the bridge between alert and relief" (Careers)
- Mission: number one global emergency response platform, delivering hope through speed, human care and precision (Careers)
- "seamless, borderless, and available through a single API" (CEO, funding news)
- "Instant medical AND SECURITY response" (older Kenya site)
- Values: Bold protectors, Real integrity, Accountability, Visionaries, Excellence (Careers)
- Calls to action: book a demo, take an AURA tour, join the response network, explore integrations (homepage)
- Stat labels: lives saved, average response time, safe places created (homepage)

## Vocabulary
- Nouns: responder, response partner, network, platform, alert, panic button, control room, system of record, integration, white-label, nationwide.
- Adjectives: vetted, trusted, verified, real-time, on-demand, fast, smart, rapid.
- Verbs: embed, integrate, connect, dispatch, protect.

## Cautions
- Terms say response times are not guaranteed (voice.md).
- Stats conflict across AURA pages (company.md).
- The Kenya page reuses South Africa copy; localise (voice.md, INFERRED).
